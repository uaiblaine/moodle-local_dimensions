The sticky footer is the hub's action surface and is not decoration: these 16 buttons open 10 of the hub's 17 modals, and for 7 of them it is the only door. Taking a button out of here does not change layout — it takes an action out of the product (and, for those 7, leaves the modal unreachable). The page has one \core\output\sticky_footer (central.php:150-152, with set_auto_enable(false) at :151) and the 3 tabs drive that same surface, swapping its innerHTML — coordinated by amd/src/central/action_footer.js.

The count — it reaches 10, it is the only door for 7
The hub builds 17 modals (4 new ModalForm + 13 Modal*.create — 6 Modal.create, 6 ModalSaveCancel.create, 1 ModalDeleteCancel.create; counted in amd/src/central/, the unit is the construction point, not the button — the point-by-point census is in modal-shell.html). The footer reaches 10 of them directly, but reaching is not monopolising: 3 of those 10 have a parallel door in the header, so exclusivity is 7.
  • 7 exclusive (the footer is the only door): move node (moveto → structure.js:988), rule (rules → rule_config.js:144), links (links → competency_links.js:862), related competencies (related → related_competencies.js:239), browse structures (browse-frameworks → competency_browser.js:106), delete-template (delete-template → plans.js:241) and participants (manage-participants → participants_manager.js:171) — each one appears only in its own footer template.
  • 3 with a parallel door — the forms: frameworks.js:173 (footer edit and header new, frameworks.mustache:82 → frameworks.js:470 → createFramework, :202-203); structure.js:798 (footer edit/addchild and header add, structure.mustache:127 → structure.js:1425-1427); plans.js:202 (footer edit-template at plans.js:724, header new-template at plans.mustache:207 → plans.js:717 and the kebab's edit-competency at plans.mustache:431 → plans.js:731 — all three land in the same openForm, plans.js:201).
Adding the nested one: only enrol_methods actually depends (enrol_methods.js:859 — the enrolment-methods tab exists only inside participants, which only opens from the footer) — 8 of the 17 depend on the footer. The scale (framework_scaleconfig.js:139) does not depend: the "Configure scale" button enters unconditionally at classes/form/framework_dynamic_form.php:195 (the $configured on line 189 only swaps the summary text), so it also appears in the create form, which comes from the header.
The 5 that never come from the footer: import (frameworks.js:249) and export CSV (frameworks.js:313), both in the Structures tab's header; competency usage (structure.js:1225, from the detail's show-usage metric), competency detail (competency_detail.js:277) and move competency from the plan's kebab (plans.js:558). Core's confirms (deleteCancelPromise, alert) do not enter this count: the hub does not build them.
The parallel door does not weaken the rule — it sharpens it. The header button creates a new entity; the footer one edits the selected one. Taking the footer one away does not orphan the modal: it orphans the action. No way is left to act on the selected row.
The CSS that holds the bar up · styles.css:5482-5514
Centre without clipping: #sticky-footer .local-dimensions-central-footer-actions (styles.css:5486-5489) applies justify-content: safe center + overflow-x: auto. The id selector is deliberate: Boost puts overflow: hidden on .sticky-footer-content nested in .stickyfooter (theme/boost/scss/moodle/sticky-footer.scss) → specificity 0,2,0; the plugin's id selector is 1,1,0 and wins without !important — which is what the rule's own comment records (styles.css:5482-5485). Without it the 7-button variant would be clipped instead of scrolling. The safe is the detail that matters: a plain center overflows on both sides when it does not fit, and what leaves on the left is unreachable by scrolling. With safe center the row stays centred while it fits and falls back to start-aligned when it does not, so the first button is always still reachable.
Where the Structure bar (the 7-button one) actually scrolls — measured in this card, not in the plugin, by really resizing the window (clamping the element alone does not do: the media query reads the viewport):
  • below ~390px — scrolls, despite the collapse: icons only, and they still do not fit (375px: 335 > 333; 326px: 57 of overflow). The narrowest windows are the ones that scroll most.
  • ~390px to 768px — fits, centred (at 390px, 348 = 348). This is the only band where the collapse "makes it fit".
  • 768px to ~950px — scrolls: the labels come back and the 7 do not fit (at 790px, scrollWidth 885 > clientWidth 733), with scrollLeft at 0 and the first button always reachable — safe in action.
  • above ~950px — fits, centred.
In other words: two scroll bands, one on each side of the breakpoint, and not just one.
sr-only collapse: below 767.98px the label becomes sr-only and only the icon is left (action-bar pattern). It is mandatory that it stays in the accessibility tree and not in display:none: the <i> are aria-hidden, so the <span> is the button's only accessible name. Both rules are reproduced in this card and hold here too.

Invariant — the footer is the launcher, not an ornament
  • Removing a button from here removes an action from the product. 11 of the 16 buttons are the entry door of a hub modal; the footer reaches 10 of the 17 and is the only door for 7 of them (8 counting enrol_methods, which lives only inside participants) — those become unreachable. The other 3 are the forms, which have a parallel door in the header: there the modal survives, but the action does not — the header creates a new entity, the footer edits the selected one, and without it no way is left to act on the chosen row. In both cases it is not a layout change.
  • Never a per-row kebab for the tab's primary entity — framework, structure node, template. The intent is the footer. If a Claude Design .dc.html shows a ⋮ on those rows, the ⋮ is a mock artefact, not a requirement: there was once a session that swapped the footer of frameworks.js for a per-card menu exactly like that, and it was reverted.
  • The rule's boundary: actions on a competency inside a plan's list are a nested list and use a kebab with reason — that tab's footer is already occupied by the 5 actions of the selected template, which is its primary entity. The ⋮ at plans.mustache:422-462 (fa fa-ellipsis-v, 5 items: edit, move up, move down, move to position, remove) is correct — do not "fix" it. Note that this kebab's "edit" (edit-competency, plans.js:731) lands in the same openForm (plans.js:201-202) — the same ModalForm as the footer's edit-template: it is one of the 3 parallel doors in the count above. The two coexist because they act on different entities — the footer on the template, the kebab on the competency in the list.
  • One surface, three owners. Moodle allows one sticky footer per page, so action_footer.js keeps a currentDispatch (:49) and a single delegated listener (:111-116); show(html, dispatch) (:68-81) swaps the innerHTML and switching tab calls hide() (:88-95), which clears it. Whoever enters re-asserts its own — core/dynamic_tabs runs init on every entry, and the guard at :127 stops re-clicking the current tab from wiping its own footer. All 3 tabs pass through here.
