The plans are created by cron. A member who joins later only gets a plan on the next run of core's scheduled task.

There are four tabs, not two, and these are the labels: central_participants_tab_cohorts, central_participants_tab_users, central_roles_tab ("Assign roles", not "Roles") and central_enrol_tab (participants_manager.mustache:40-73). The two marked with ·cap are conditional: canassignroles and canmanageenrol — whoever lacks the capability sees only the first two, and then the two-tab strip is a legitimate render. They are nav nav-tabs without .dynamictabs: no icon and no accent indicator — the active tab is plain Boost's "folder", as drawn.
The body is the add autocomplete plus a 4-column table — Cohort (cohort, a core string), Members, Plans, Actions (cohort_manager.mustache:42-45; the whole table at :38-49, with a visually-hidden caption). The rows are built on the client by makeRow() (cohort_manager.js:51-74): name in <th scope="row">, the two numbers, and two btn-outline-secondary btn-sm buttons with visible text — fa-exchange "Sync" and fa-trash "Remove cohort" (action_button.js:38-49). With no cohort attached, a cell with colSpan 4 says "No cohorts attached." (cohort_manager.js:91-100).
Each row acts on its own, immediately. Adding fires local_dimensions_add_template_cohort, shows the queueing toast and re-renders the body to reset the autocomplete (cohort_manager.js:166-181); the row's ⇄ calls local_dimensions_sync_template_cohort and toasts the same text (:152-157); the 🗑 asks for confirmation through saveCancelPromise, removes and returns focus to the list (:111-134). There is no apply button, and no batch.
All the WS returns is success. add_template_cohort::execute_returns() declares a single boolean field (add_template_cohort.php:99-103); the syncqueued => true travels in the other of the audit event template_cohort_added (:81-89), not in the response. The queueing itself is sync_task::queue() (:78), with dedupe queue_adhoc_task($task, true) (sync_template_cohort.php:60) because attaching and clicking sync queue the same pair and competency_plan has no unique index on (templateid, userid). The adhoc task calls api::create_plans_from_template_cohort() only after reconfirming the template and the link (:86-92).
The link itself is core's: the table is competency_templatecohort, handled by the persistent core_competency\template_cohort. The two numbers per row come from local_dimensions_list_template_cohorts: members is the count of cohort_members and plans is how many plans of this template exist for members of the cohort (list_template_cohorts.php:79-92, declared at :105-108) — which is why the two diverge until cron has run.
