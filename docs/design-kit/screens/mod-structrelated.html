MOD.STRUCTREL-MODAL · detail.js:277-283 · core/modal · large · show · removeOnClose MOD.STRUCTREL-CARD · modal.mustache:36 · the card IS the dialog MOD.STRUCTREL-CLOSE · modal.mustache:37-40 · colour = textcolor (detail.js:294) MOD.STRUCTREL-CONTENT · modal.mustache:41-45 · linksclickable/showrelated OFF
The card is the dialog. The JS puts the class local-dimensions-related-modal on the modal's root (detail.js:285) and it rewrites the core/modal shell: the .modal-header becomes display:none (styles.css:6671-6673), the .modal-content gets border:0; background:transparent; box-shadow:none (:6675-6679), the .modal-body zeroes its padding (:6681-6683) and the .modal-dialog fits in 620px with a 24px radius (:6663-6668). The radius on the dialog is not decoration: core/modal focuses the .modal-dialog (tabindex 0) on opening, and since content and body add no padding the focus outline hugs the card — rounding it at 24px is what makes the ring match the card, and the comment in the source says so (:6666-6667). The title is passed to Modal.create (detail.js:278), but the hidden header swallows it; the name lives in the card (content.mustache:48).
One shell, two hosts — and that is why the visuals are identical. This modal's body is the same partial as the Competencies tab's inline detail panel: modal.mustache:43 only wraps central/structure_detail_content. Nothing here is a second artwork of the same card — the difference between the two surfaces fits into two flags, passed at render time (detail.js:274-276).
Both locks are on purpose. linksclickable:false makes the three counters come out as <div> instead of <button> (content.mustache:78-86, :90-98, :102-110) — otherwise a click would open MOD.USAGE on top of this modal. The CSS confirms the split: only button.local-dimensions-central-structure-metric-value gets a cursor, an underline on hover and a focus ring (styles.css:6561-6574), and the comment there says the related-competency modal draws the same counters as a static number. showrelated:false omits the whole related-competencies section (content.mustache:116-125) — otherwise a chip would open another MOD.STRUCTREL. It is read-only: peeking, not editing (the editor is MOD.RELATED).
The lock is also id discipline, not just click discipline. With linksclickable false the template stops emitting the metric labels' ids and the values' aria-labelledby wiring (content.mustache:77, :89, :101; the docblock states the reason at :30-31) — so the two surfaces can coexist on the same page without colliding on a duplicate id. The same discipline names the collapsible description: renderDetailInto receives an id scope ('tree' in the panel, 'modal' here, detail.js:291) and it enters the partial's id (detail.js:198).
The close button is its own, not core's. Core's .btn-close is hidden along with the header, and the global restyle of the blue close chip excludes this modal through :not(.local-dimensions-related-modal) in all three selectors (styles.css:5074, :5088, :5098-5099). Its own button is 36×36, radius 10, translucent white (:6690-6707), and its glyph colour comes from the competency's textcolor in JS, with #fff as the fallback (detail.js:294) — so it contrasts with the coloured header, whatever that header is. It is also what closes: the handler calls modal.hide() directly (detail.js:295), with no core wiring.
The header's colour is the competency's; what sits on top of it is not. The gradient is 140deg in three stops fed by --ld-plans-hdr-0/48/100 (styles.css:5829), which the JS writes from the bgcolor and from darkenHex at 0.16 and 0.34 (detail.js:128-130, the function at :102-112); the header's text colour is the textcolor (detail.js:136) over the #fff the CSS assumes (styles.css:6501-6503). With no bgcolor the JS removes the three properties and the gradient falls back to the brand default (detail.js:131-135). What does not follow the competency: the taxonomy badge and the rule chip stay on the fixed slate #495057 with white text (styles.css:6513-6524, :5976-5981), and the "glass" chips are translucent white with color:inherit (:5983-5987) — they inherit the colour painted on the header instead of fixing their own. The prefix of the identification chip is core's string idnumber (content.mustache:59), so the label follows the site's language.
The rule chip has a condition the screen shows satisfied. It appears only when the node has children and has a rule — data.haschildren === '1' && (data.ruletype || '') !== '' (detail.js:153-154) —, so a leaf never shows it. The text is the string central_structure_rule with the rule's label interpolated (detail.js:156), and the label chip's is central_plans_labelchip with the Type field (detail.js:165). A chip with no value does not sit empty: it disappears — setChip hides the wrapper when the value is '' (detail.js:69-78).
The data comes fresh, and the returns list declares everything the card paints. The modal does not reuse the tree row's dataset: it re-reads the node through local_dimensions_get_structure_node (detail.js:266-269) and maps the response with nodeToDetailData (:235-253); if the node does not come back, nothing opens (:270-272). And the allowlist trap is closed: the execute_returns() reuses browse_structure::node_structure() (get_structure_node.php:105), which declares taxonomy, idnumber, scale, description, type, tag1, tag2, bgcolor, textcolor, coursecount, activitycount, templatecount, haschildren, ruletype and rulelabel — so clean_returnvalue silences no field of the card. The three numbers fall back to '0' when absent (detail.js:180-182). Since the modal is removeOnClose, the asynchronous renders (chips and description) are guarded by an isConnected passed in as a guard (detail.js:291).
Who opens it: two doors, one function. The related-competency chip in the Competencies tab's panel (data-action="open-related", structure_related_chips.mustache:37-39 → structure.js:1245-1248, with the selector at :73) and the clickable competency name in the Learning plans tab (data-action="open-competency-detail", plans.js:743-744). Both call the same openCompetencyDetailModal (detail.js:265) — and that is what makes this card the canonical portrait of a competency in any tab of the hub.
Fixed light surface — a faithful portrait, not preview sloppiness. The literals of the card's body are light and have no dark variant in the plugin: #fff in the body (styles.css:5997), #fbfcfd with a #e7e9ec border on the metric cards (:6540-6542) and #6a737b/#0f6cbf on the label/value pair (:6547, :6555). The only prefers-color-scheme in styles.css is scoped to the contrast panel of the competency form (:400), and it does not reach this card. That is why the preview keeps the card on the shipped literals in both themes and dresses only the page frame in DS tokens: changing the card's colour here would invent a behaviour that does not exist.
