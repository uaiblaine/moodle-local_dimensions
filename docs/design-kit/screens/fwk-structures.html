It adds LOADING_CLASS (:44) + aria-busy (:78-79) and clears them in a finally (:100-106), under a monotonic per-pane generation counter (:74-75) so an old reload cannot switch off a newer one's spin.
This tab is the best case of the change: its 6 calls are 100% of the CRUD — saving (openFrameworkForm, frameworks.js:180), importing (openImportForm, :270), visibility (toggleVisibility, :374), duplicating (duplicateFramework, :386), deleting (deleteFramework, :419) and the hidden toggle (:522) — and none of them is in place. The refreshNode caveat that constrains EST does not exist here: on this tab every action reloads the pane and every reload shows the curtain.

FWK-IMP-BANNER
This is the only in-body loading treatment the plugin ships — alert alert-info + spinner-border spinner-border-sm, assembled by showImportLoading (frameworks.js:211-225): the banner receives data-region="import-loading" (:229), the alert classes (:230) and role="status" (:231), and the label central_frameworks_importing enters as a real text <span> (:232-234). There is a duplicate guard (:224) and removal when the server's validation hands the form back (hideImportLoading, :244-250). Fired on SUBMIT_BUTTON_PRESSED (:265). The ring above really does spin.
The ARIA here is right — it is the export's that is not. makeSpinner() (central/download.js:53-58 since 2026-07-28, when the Plans tab's own export started sharing it; it was frameworks.js) puts aria-hidden="true" on the ring, and what carries the role="status" and the text is the banner (frameworks.js:220, :223) — name on the container, decorative ring, exactly the form states.html asks for. The export loader reuses the same makeSpinner() (:284) but its container is a <span data-region="export-loader" class="d-inline-flex" hidden> with no role and no accessible name (frameworks_export.mustache:41) — so the download state announces nothing. It is the only point on the screen where states.html's loading pattern is not met. The Plans tab's export loader is the corrected shape and can be copied from: plans_export.mustache:55-57 gives the span a role="status" and a visually-hidden label, and drops d-inline-flex — which is display:inline-flex !important on Bootstrap 4 and would beat [hidden], so the loader here could never hide on 4.5 in the first place.
