Dropped: TRK-CHIP-COMP, the tracker's competency-field chip group. It was built from the page's single competency, so every card carried the same value and pressing a chip matched all cards or none. Retired with its setting: db/upgrade.php:452-463 unsets viewcompetency_filter_fields_competency, and no such setting is registered in settings.php any more. The tracker's chips are course fields only; competency fields survive on the plan overview as OVW-CHIP.
Placement is the one thing that changed: the always-visible chip bar under the hero is gone, but the component itself is untouched — chip_filters.mustache still renders the group label, the platter of aria-pressed buttons and the clear button, and filter_tabs_nav.js still supplies the paddles, the sliding indicator and the arrow-key nav. The toolbars that host the button are documented in trk-tracker and ovw-overview.
